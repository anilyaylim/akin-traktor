FAQ

Frequently asked questions

I don’t know the part code. What should I do?

Send a photo of the part and your tractor’s make and model on WhatsApp. Our specialists match it to the right stock code.

Which tractor brands do you supply?

We stock parts for Ford, Massey Ferguson, UTB, Steyr, 50NC (Fiat) and Tümosan. Every product page lists the compatible models.

How do I get a price?

Add the parts to your quote list and send it by email or WhatsApp. Price and lead time come back the same day. Dealers see prices on the B2B platform.

How fast do you ship?

Parts in stock ship the same day. Heavy parts go by freight, with a tracking number.

How can I become a dealer?

Write to us via the contact form with the subject “Dealership”; our team will share the terms and B2B access.
